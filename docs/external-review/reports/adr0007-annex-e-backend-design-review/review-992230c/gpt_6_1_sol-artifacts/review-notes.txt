Additional normative defect, R2-05:
- ADR line166 explicitly says negative expiration means no ticket, which is
  backwards: expiration=-60 requests ticket-generation semantics.
- Do not claim that LoadExternal(RH_NULL) forces PolicySigned output to have an empty digest.
  The final rev1.59 reference code calls TicketComputeAuth for negative
  expiration; HierarchyGetProof defaults to nullProof for RH_NULL and
  TicketComputeAuth computes a digest. Not allowing PolicyTicket is distinct
  from incorrectly claiming no ticket is produced. Preserve -60 and discard
  its output ticket, or choose positive expiration if no ticket is intended.
