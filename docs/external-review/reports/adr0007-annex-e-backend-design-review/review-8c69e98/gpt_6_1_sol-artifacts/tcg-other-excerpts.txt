TCG EK Credential Profile v2.5 R2
https://trustedcomputinggroup.org/wp-content/uploads/TCG-EK-Credential-Profile-V-2.5-R2_published.pdf

"If the EK Template is Absent, the default template is used as the TPMT_PUBLIC."
"If the EK Template is Populated and the EK Nonce is Absent, the EK Template is used unmodified as the TPMT_PUBLIC."
"Read all NV index handles or those of interest returned in the list from step 1 using TPM2_NV_ReadPublic(), and TPM2_NV_Read()."
"An EK Certificate is stored in an NV Index as an X.509 certificate encoded in DER format. The NV Index data contains only the DER certificate data."

عند وجود nonce لـRSA 2048، يُحشى إلى 256 بايتًا ويُدخل في unique.rsa؛ وعند ECC P256، يُحشى إلى 32 في unique.ecc.x، ويكون y أصفارًا بطول 32.
قراءة بيانات القالب/nonce تسبق إنشاء EK بواسطة TPM2_CreatePrimary.

TCG TPM 2.0 r1.59 Part 2
https://trustedcomputinggroup.org/wp-content/uploads/TCG_TPM2_r1p59_Part2_Structures_pub.pdf

§10.7.2:
"When a command requires a ticket and no ticket is available, the caller is required to provide a structure with a ticket tag that is correct for the context. The hierarchy shall be set to TPM_RH_NULL, and digest shall be the Empty Buffer (a buffer with a size field of zero). This construct is the NULL Ticket."
